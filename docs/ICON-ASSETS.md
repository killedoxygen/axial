# Delivery Assets

`assets/brand-mark.json` is the only Axial mark geometry and color source. Its
`schema_version` changes only when the file contract changes; `design_revision`
changes when approved geometry or colors change. `frontend/src/ui/Logo.tsx`
renders that source directly.

The mark is an axolotl face in three parts: `face`, `left_gills` and
`right_gills`. `background` is the app-icon plate and sets the icon bounds, so
it extends past `view_box` to give the mark its margin.

## Generated icons

The exact Tauri CLI version in `toolchain.json` renders these selected outputs:

- `apps/desktop/icons/icon.png`
- `apps/desktop/icons/icon.ico`
- `apps/desktop/icons/dev/icon.png`
- `apps/desktop/icons/dev/icon.ico`
- `frontend/static/favicon.png`

Generate and verify them through the only supported entry points:

```sh
task assets:generate
task assets:check
```

Generation uses a unique repository-local `tmp/assets-*` stage. Existing
destinations are revalidated, copied to verified backups, and atomically
replaced one file at a time. `assets/provenance.json` is published last as the
transaction's commit marker. Any ordinary pre-marker failure restores completed
files in reverse order. A hard stop can leave a complete old/new mix and an
ignored stage, but cannot remove the last complete destination. The lightweight
verifier blocks build, development, and packaging while any tracked hash is
stale or any untracked delivery media exists; rerunning generation repairs an
interrupted pre-marker publication. Check mode generates twice, proves selected
Tauri outputs are byte-deterministic, compares them to the tracked destinations,
and changes no tracked file.

The two approved macOS Composer products are retained as strict-hash inputs:

- `apps/desktop/icons/macos/icon.icns`
- `apps/desktop/icons/dev/macos/icon.icns`

They are not regenerated by the portable pipeline. Replace them only after
explicit visual approval and native macOS packaging verification.

## Promotional art

`docs/brand/` holds the README banner (`banner.svg`, with a `banner.png`
raster at twice its size) and the 1280 by 640 social card
(`social-preview.svg` and `.png`). Both draw the mark from
`assets/brand-mark.json` in the five accent presets and set their text as
Manrope outlines, so they render the same without the font. Redraw them when
`design_revision` changes. GitHub's social preview is uploaded by hand in the
repository settings.

## Loader marks

`vanilla_icon.svg`, `fabric_icon.svg`, `forge_icon.svg`, `neoforge_icon.svg`
and `quilt_icon.svg` under `frontend/static/` are repository-authored redrawings
of each loader's own logo: the creeper's face, Fabric's length of cloth,
Forge's anvil, NeoForge's fox, and Quilt's nine patches with one turned. They
are rendered as monochrome CSS masks in creation tiles, downloads and instance
tiles, so the theme owns their colour.

The five are one set and are redrawn together:

- A 24 by 24 view box that the art fills edge to edge on its longer axis, with
  no per-loader offsets in CSS. Each mark is centred on its mass, not its
  bounds: Quilt on its three by three grid with only the turned patch reaching
  past it, the anvil on its centre of area so the long horn does not pull the
  body off centre.
- Solid `evenodd` paths with no colour or stroke. A mark is one path at full
  strength, or two: a first path at `opacity="0.6"` and a full one over it.
- Geometry comes from the logo. The creeper sits on a six by six module of 4.
  Fabric and the fox keep their logos' cell maps (13 by 14 and 12 by 13 cells)
  with every step rounded. The anvil follows Forge's outline: long horn, one
  slit, heel carried past the body, spread feet.
- Two strengths are only for a logo whose own artwork plainly contrasts light
  and dark regions. Today that is the fox alone: its ears, eye whites and
  muzzle are full and its face is 0.6, with no seam between them, and its
  black pupils and nose stay open. Do not add a second strength to a logo that
  does not show the difference itself.
- The other four are one strength. Where their parts need telling apart they
  are separate pieces set apart by a seam: Fabric's turned end and Quilt's
  patches.

Keep each loader recognisable as its logo; do not replace a mark with a neutral
glyph or a generic drawing of the subject. They are Axial's drawings, not the
projects' official artwork, and are used only to name the loader an instance
runs. The loaders' names and logos remain their owners'.

## Interface icons

The interface icon registry in `frontend/src/ui/Icons.tsx` exposes 52 semantic
names backed by exact `lucide-preact@1.25.0`. UI component contracts accept the
closed `IconName` union; backend and domain strings remain untyped until a UI
presenter maps them to that union. The `stroke` prop maps directly to Lucide's
`strokeWidth`, preserving intentional per-surface weight adjustments.

The byte-exact upstream ISC/MIT notice ships at
`frontend/static/licenses/Lucide-ISC.txt`. To refresh Lucide, update the exact
manifest and lockfile version, immutable provenance revision and hashes, notice,
52-name contract review, and visual smoke results together.

## Worlds empty art

The Worlds empty state uses the repository-authored masks
`frontend/static/worlds-empty-base.svg` and

separate base/accent geometry so theme colors remain CSS-owned.

## Third-party assets

`assets/provenance.json` is the strict source, rights, revision, mode, refresh,
generator, and exact-hash inventory for retained delivery assets. It covers the
tracked desktop icons, frontend font files and notices, SND kit files, loader
marks, Worlds empty-state masks, interface icon notice, favicon, and
Microsoft authentication symbol.

The Microsoft symbol is the byte-exact asset published with Microsoft identity
branding guidance. It is used only inside Microsoft authentication controls;
the policy record is not a general software-license claim. SND terms must be
reviewed again whenever the pinned kit is refreshed. Runtime-downloaded media,
including account skins, is owned by its runtime media contract rather than
this static delivery inventory.
